# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses or improves
it – home owners, installers and developers alike, whatever their experience.

- Be friendly and patient. Many people here are setting up EEBUS for the first
  time; there are no stupid questions.
- Assume good intent and stay constructive in reviews and issues.
- Respect different viewpoints, languages and levels of experience.
- Do not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone, and do not publish
  other people's private data (that includes device serial numbers, SKIs or
  logs of other people's systems without their consent).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
[@CoreTex](https://github.com/CoreTex) via GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code and issues that do not follow this code of conduct, and may block
contributors who repeatedly do so.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
